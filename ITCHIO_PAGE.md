## 📦 Distribution Packages

1. **Windows Standalone**: `dist/SportsmansQuest_Windows_ReadyToPlay.zip` (82 MB)
   - Contains self-contained `SportsmansQuest.exe` and `SportsmansQuest.pck`.
   - Double-click to play immediately.
2. **Web Release**: `dist/SportsmansQuest_Web.zip` (2.5 MB)
   - Contains WebGL/WebAssembly HTML5 build files.
   - Embeds into Itch.io in-browser player.
3. **Ultra-Lite Core PCK**: `dist/SportsmansQuest.pck` (~240 KB)
   - Pure procedural math package.
